## Available Skills

### [client-need-distillation](skills/client-need-distillation/SKILL.md)

把客户沟通速记与碎片反馈蒸馏为可落地、高 ROI 的 AI 试点场景。

Distills fragmented client communications (meeting notes, chat logs) into shippable, high-ROI AI pilot scenarios. Built from a real FDE engagement with a hazardous-chemical logistics company, generalized into a repeatable workflow:

1. **Evidence audit** — tag every input as client-quote / archive / inference
2. **Speednote → scenario cards** — who / current state / pain / AI role / data deps / measurability
3. **Scenario map** — heat × data availability × measurability × org friction → tiers
4. **Client-first lens** (mandatory) — who is the client, what did they actually buy, are your KPIs inverted? Service outcomes as primary metrics; internal efficiency (FTE/hours) demoted to secondary economics
5. **Net-new services scan** — what was previously uneconomic for humans that AI now makes viable
6. **N-role thought experiment** — "if the company kept only 3 roles, who survives?" The rest restructure into an agent network (client-facing interfaces, not internal tools)
7. **One-week quick win** — pick ONE scenario: landable in 7 days, observable ROI, client-facing; then roll forward

Core stance: **capabilities stay invisible; solutions are what users see.**
